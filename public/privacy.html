DropKit Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Compliant with Indian IT Act 2000 & Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act

1. Information We Collect

DropKit strictly collects only minimal information necessary to deliver your digital publications and reconcile payment transactions:

  • Customer Name: To personalize your digital product license receipt.
  • Email Address: To deliver time-limited download entitlement tokens and purchase receipts.
  • Mobile Number (Optional): For UPI payment SMS notifications.
  • Transaction Metadata: Order ID, amount paid in INR, UPI payment reference ID, and download access logs.

2. What We Explicitly NEVER Collect

We prioritize buyer confidentiality. DropKit:

  • NEVER collects Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, or government identity cards.
  • NEVER collects or stores bank account passwords, UPI MPINs, or debit/credit card CVVs.
  • NEVER sells or trades customer email addresses to third-party ad networks.

3. UPI Payment Processing

Dynamic UPI QR transactions are routed through NPCI-authorized payment aggregators (such as Razorpay / ICICI). The payment verification is performed server-side using cryptographic webhook signatures.

4. Data Deletion & Privacy Rights

You may request permanent deletion of your customer records from our database by emailing privacy@dropkit.io or contacting the merchant administrator.

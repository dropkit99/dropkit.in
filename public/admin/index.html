⚙️ Auto-Switching Architecture Rules

  • One Global Active Account: Every customer visiting permanent URLs sees the same single active merchant method.
  • Threshold Auto-Switch: When confirmed collections reach the configured limit, the engine switches atomically to the next verified account by priority.
  • Order Immutability: Once an order is created, its assigned merchant account and UPI ID are frozen forever. Future switches never alter existing orders.
  • Safe Fallback: If all accounts reach their limit, new checkouts are paused with a clear maintenance notice rather than generating broken QR codes.
